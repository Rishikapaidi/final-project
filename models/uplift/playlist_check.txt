UPLIFT PLAYLIST CHECK (structure of the 5-song path; not an effect on any listener)
Readings: app.py combine_emotions with HSEmotion's fusion settings on the 6,865 distinct (face, comment) test pairs of
scripts/evaluate_fusion.py; 3,825 have sad + angry + fear + disgust >= 50% (Uplift applies).
Readings per start emotion (available -> sampled, seed 42): sad 952 -> 200, angry 1219 -> 200, fear 933 -> 200, disgust 721 -> 200
Each reading's playlist mix (emotions at >= 15%), its start point and waypoints come from the app's code unchanged.
Songs: only those with a saved preview (data/songs/previews.csv), as the app plays them by default.

  (a mixed reading starts between the centres of its emotions, weighted by their share)

COLUMNS: n paths | 5 songs = path has 5 songs | distinct = 5 different title+artist | d1 med = median distance of song 1
from the start point (in Muse standard deviations of valence / arousal) | 1st=start = song 1 is in the start emotion's
bucket | 1st in mix = song 1 is in the bucket of one of the reading's emotions (>= 15%) | V steps / A steps = share of the 4 steps that move in the planned direction | V all4 / A all4 = all 4 steps do
(strict) | dV, dA = song 5 minus song 1 (mean, sd) | brighter = song 5 has higher valence than song 1 | calmer = lower
arousal. Step and change columns use complete paths only. For sad the planned arousal goes down, then up, and the
last song is meant to be MORE energetic (happy), so 'calmer' is not the aim there.

== WEAK SPOTS (from the numbers above)
- Telugu and Malayalam: the app does not offer Uplift (too few playable songs near the waypoints); for them
    Telugu    sad      complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 70%, median song-1 distance 0.22, valence steps right 88%
    Telugu    angry    complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 29%, median song-1 distance 0.49, valence steps right 72%
    Telugu    fear     complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 74%, median song-1 distance 0.28, valence steps right 75%
    Telugu    disgust  complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 88%, median song-1 distance 0.19, valence steps right 79%
    Malayalam sad      complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 60%, median song-1 distance 0.21, valence steps right 92%
    Malayalam angry    complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 25%, median song-1 distance 0.54, valence steps right 63%
    Malayalam fear     complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 77%, median song-1 distance 0.36, valence steps right 76%
    Malayalam disgust  complete 100%, song 1 in the start bucket 88%, median song-1 distance 0.12, valence steps right 85%
- Hindi angry: song 1 in the start bucket only 42% (in one of the reading's emotions 85%)
- Korean angry: song 1 in the start bucket only 46% (in one of the reading's emotions 88%)
- Per-step direction is strict (any step against the plan counts as wrong, however small). The planned arousal
  steps are small for disgust (about -0.12 per step) and for the first half of sad (about -0.14, then +1.0), well
  within the spread of one bucket, so 'A all4' is low there by construction; dA is the better measure.
- A reading with several heavy emotions starts between their centres, so song 1 can come from another emotion of
  the reading's mix: this is how angry's '1st=start' is low while '1st in mix' stays high.
- The 4 negative buckets overlap in valence / arousal (data/README.md), so 'close to the start mood' is measured
  in valence / arousal and by bucket; neither says the song sounds sad, angry, fearful or disgusted to a person.
- Valence / arousal are dataset values: Muse listener tags (English) and Spotify audio features mapped onto
  the Muse scale by percentile (other languages). They were not checked by people. This checks the playlist's
  structure, not any effect on a listener.
